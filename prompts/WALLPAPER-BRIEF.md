# Yautja theme wallpaper brief

Wallpapers for an Omarchy (Hyprland) desktop theme built around thermal vision:
an unseen hunter watching a jungle through an infrared imager.

## Palette

False-colour thermal ramp, cold to hot:

- `#060818` near-black indigo (theme background, should dominate)
- `#1A2470` cold blue, `#5A0FA8` violet, `#C2188B` magenta
- `#FF3B2F` red, `#FF8A1F` orange, `#FFD91A` yellow, `#FFFBE8` white-hot (small areas only)

## Rules for every image

- Original imagery only. No film or game characters, creatures, masks, armour or
  logos, and nothing that reads as a likeness of one.
- No text, letters, glyphs, numbers, UI, HUD frames, watermarks or borders.
- At least 60% of the frame stays dark and calm so terminal windows sit on it
  comfortably. The top 8% stays quiet for the status bar.
- Hot colours are the accent, not the fill.
- Rich detail and real texture: foliage, mist, sensor grain. Not flat vector blobs.

## Layouts

1. `01-heat-signature` Dense jungle seen through a thermal imager. Foliage is cold
   blue and violet. One small, distant human heat signature (a soldier seen from
   behind or in profile, anonymous) glows orange to white in the right third. The
   left two thirds are cold canopy.
2. `02-tri-laser` Night jungle, heavy mist. Three thin red laser beams cut through
   the mist from the upper left and end as three dots in a tight triangle on a
   wet tree trunk, right of centre. Almost everything else is indigo darkness.
3. `03-cloak` A waterfall and jungle clearing in thermal blues and violets. In the
   centre-left, a barely visible refraction shimmer in the outline of a tall
   standing figure bends the foliage behind it. No body detail at all, only the
   distortion and a faint magenta rim.
4. `04-isotherm` Abstract. A thermal topography of flowing isotherm contour bands,
   cold indigo across most of the frame, one hot bloom low in the right corner.
   No subject. The calm everyday option.

## Sizes

Each layout is composed for each aspect ratio, not cropped from another one:
the subject keeps its place and the scene extends sideways or vertically.
